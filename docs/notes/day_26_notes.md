# Day 26 Notes

## Objective

Generate temporal change products using Sentinel-2 imagery from 2021 and 2026.

## Outputs

- NDVI 2021
- NDVI 2026
- NDVI Change

- NDBI 2021
- NDBI 2026
- NDBI Change

- Vegetation Loss Mask
- Urban Growth Mask

## Key Idea

Vegetation Loss:

NDVI(2026) - NDVI(2021)

Urban Growth:

NDBI(2026) - NDBI(2021)

## Deliverables

First GeoSentinel change-detection products.
